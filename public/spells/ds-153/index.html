ds-153 / Layout / Markup

Intrinsic Disclosure Width (`calc-size()`)

Expand a compact disclosure to its real max-content width plus breathing room. The fixed-width fallback stays usable where `calc-size()` is unavailable.

The spell and its demo use zero JavaScript. Optional copy controls do not affect the example.
