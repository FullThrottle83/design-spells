THE STUDIO HANDBOOK / 7 minute read

A better beginning.

A practical guide to starting creative work with clarity, care and enough room to think.

01

Start with what matters.

Choose one project you can finish, not five you can only start. Write down the change you want someone to feel when the work is done. That sentence will guide every decision that follows.

02

Make room for the work.

Protect a quiet hour. Gather your references, close the extra tabs, and place the tools you need within reach. A thoughtful environment is not a luxury; it makes your next action easier.

03

Leave a clear handover.

A finished file is only part of the work. Explain what changed, what remains open, and where the next person should begin. Kind documentation lets good work keep moving.
