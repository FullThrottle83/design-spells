Perforated Ticket Card

A ticket/coupon card with real perforated “holes” on the edges via pseudo-elements.

The spell and its demo use zero JavaScript. Optional copy controls do not affect the example.

<article class="ticket">
  <div class="ticket-body">
    <h3>Summer offer</h3>
    <p>20% off every annual plan.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ticket-stub">
    <strong>−20%</strong>
    <span>Code: SUMMER26</span>
  </div>
