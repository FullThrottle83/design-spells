Auto-Inverting Contrast Scrim (`mix-blend-mode`)

Text or icons that automatically invert over the images or patterns behind them. **WCAG warning:** Maximum contrast is guaranteed against black or white surfaces. Avoid 50% mid-grey backgrounds where a 4.5:1 contrast ratio cannot be guaranteed. Add `isolation: isolate` on the parent container so the blend mode does not leak to the whole page.

The spell and its demo use zero JavaScript. Optional copy controls do not affect the example.
